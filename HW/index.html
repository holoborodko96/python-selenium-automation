How to cook borshch soup

Ingridients:

  • Beef - 500 g
  • Beetroot - 1 pc.
  • Potatoes - 2 pcs.
  • White cabbage - 200 g
  • Carrot - 1 pc.
  • Onion - 1 pc.
  • Tomato paste - 1 tbsp.
  • Vegetable oil - 2 tbsp.
  • Vinegar - 1 tbsp.
  • Bay leaf - 1 pc.
  • Black pepper with peas - 2-3 pcs.
  • Salt - 2 tsp.(to taste)
  • Water - 1.5 l
  • Green dill and/or parsley (for serving) - 3-4 sprigs
  • Sour cream (for serving) - 2 tbsp.

Instructions:

  1. Prepare products.
  2. Cut the beef into large pieces.
  3. Pour cold water over the meat in a saucepan, bring to a boil. Boil the broth for about 1.5 hours on low heat.
  4. Add salt at the end of cooking.
  5. Peel the beets, cut into strips.
  6. Peel the carrot, grate it on a coarse grater.
  7. Peel the onion, finely chop.
  8. Chop the cabbage.
  9. Peel the potatoes, cut into cubes.
  10. Heat vegetable oil in a pan. Put the beets, carrots and onions in a pan and stew over medium heat (simmer), stirring, for 5-7 minutes.
  11. At the end, add vinegar and tomato paste. Mix Cook the vegetables for another 3-4 minutes, stirring.
  12. Put potatoes and cabbage in boiling broth, cook for 10 minutes. (Young cabbage is added 5 minutes before the end of cooking borscht.) Then add sautéed vegetables, bay leaf and pepper. Cook borscht with beef for another 5-7 minutes.
  13. Let the finished borscht infuse for 10-15 minutes.
  14. Cut the greens.
  15. Pour the borscht into plates, season with sour cream and sprinkle with greens.
